[size=5][b]Aldrich Minimap[/b][/size]
A minimap of the surface of your map, always in the corner of the screen.

[b]Tested on Going Medieval 1.1.19.[/b]

[size=4][b]Features[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Ground coloured by what is on it: grass, dirt, sand, clay, gravel, rock, snow, fields and roofs, with the relief shaded on top.
[*]Water in three shades, by depth.
[*]Everything alive as a coloured dot: settlers, visitors, prisoners, enemies, predators, livestock and wildlife.
[*]The camera's view drawn on the map. [b]Click or drag[/b] on it to move the camera there.
[*][b]+ / -[/b] buttons to zoom from 1x to 6x, kept centred on the camera.
[*][b]Hover[/b] to read the name of the ground or water under the cursor, in the game's own language.
[*][b]Ctrl+M[/b] shows and hides it.
[*]Size, corner, position, zoom and key can be changed in the config file.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Note[/b][/size]
When a game is loaded, the map can take [b]between 1 and 10 seconds[/b] to show correctly: it waits for the terrain and the water to finish loading and repaints itself a few times while they do. This is normal.

[size=4][b]Requirements[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]BepInEx 5 (x64)[/b] installed in the Going Medieval folder.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Installation[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Install BepInEx 5 (x64) in the Going Medieval folder, if you have not already.
[*]Extract the archive into the Going Medieval folder (where [i]Going Medieval.exe[/i] is), so the file ends up at:
[code]Going Medieval\BepInEx\plugins\AldrichMinimap\AldrichMinimap.dll[/code]
[*]Start the game and load a map.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Settings[/b][/size]
After the first launch:
[code]Going Medieval\BepInEx\config\aldrich.minimap.cfg[/code]
[list]
[*][b]Size[/b] - side of the map, in pixels of a 1080p screen.
[*][b]Corner[/b] - TopRight (default, left of the notifications) or BottomRight.
[*][b]TopOffsetX / TopOffsetY[/b] - position when in the top right.
[*][b]OffsetX / OffsetY[/b] - position when in the bottom right.
[*][b]Zoom[/b] - 1 to 6.
[*][b]ToggleKey[/b] - default M + LeftControl.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Uninstall[/b][/size]
Delete the [i]AldrichMinimap[/i] folder from [i]BepInEx\plugins[/i]. The mod does not touch your saves: it can be added or removed at any time.

[size=4][b]Compatibility[/b][/size]
It only draws on top of the screen and reads the map; it does not change the game. It should work alongside other mods.
