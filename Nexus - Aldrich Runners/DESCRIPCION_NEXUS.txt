[size=5][b]Aldrich - The Runner[/b][/size]
Something came down with the last caravan, and it did not stay in the body it arrived in.

[b]Tested on Going Medieval 1.1.19.[/b] English and Spanish texts.

[size=4][b]Features[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]The Runner[/b] - quadruped, fast and tough. Hunts in packs, does not flee, and goes through a palisade rather than around it.
[*][b]Climbs walls[/b] - a Runner that cannot reach a settler climbs the wall in front of it, as many levels as it takes.
[*][b]Acid blood[/b] - hit it up close and it burns you, splashes whoever stands next to it, and leaves a pool when it dies.
[*][b]The tail[/b] - it bites the one in front and lashes whoever comes from behind.
[*][b]Eggs and Spider Aliens[/b] - eggs breathe, then open; the Spider Alien leaps onto a face and knocks the victim down.
[*][b]Fear[/b] - unrecruited settlers near a Runner drop what they are doing and run, and morale sinks.
[*][b]Its own remains[/b] - every creature leaves its own carcass. Butcher a Runner for carapace and bones - there is no meat on it.
[/list]

[size=4][b]How it works[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]The Runner[/b] - 110 health (between a boar and a bear), fast, hunts in packs and never flees.
[*][b]Climbing[/b] - if it cannot get closer to a settler it climbs the wall in its way, up to 12 levels, and drops over or waits on top.
[*][b]Acid blood[/b] - whoever wounds it in melee has a 50% chance of an acid burn that keeps eating until tended; bystanders get a third of that, and the pool it leaves when it dies a third again.
[*][b]Tail[/b] - lashes anything alive within 2.5 tiles that is not its target: 6 damage every 3.5 seconds.
[*][b]Dread and panic[/b] - within 14 tiles settlers get -10 mood and work slower; within 6 tiles, -20 mood and unrecruited settlers drop their work and run. Your drafted soldiers hold their ground.
[*][b]Spider Aliens[/b] - they hatch from eggs and leap onto a face. The victim falls unconscious and does not starve, freeze or dry out... for [b]48 game hours[/b]. Then something comes out of the chest. Kill the Spider Alien first to free them.
[*][b]Remains[/b] - Runner, Spider Alien and egg each leave their own carcass; butcher them for carapace and bones. No meat.
[/list]
Every number above can be changed in [i]BepInEx\config\aldrich.xenomorph_runner.cfg[/i].

[size=4][b]Requirements[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]BepInEx 5 (x64)[/b]. Vortex installs it for you with Going Medieval.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Installation[/b][/size]
[b]With Vortex:[/b] install it like any other mod.

[b]By hand:[/b]
[list=1]
[*]Install BepInEx 5 (x64) in the Going Medieval folder, if you have not already.
[*]Extract the archive into the Going Medieval folder (where [i]Going Medieval.exe[/i] is), so the file ends up at:
[code]Going Medieval\BepInEx\plugins\AldrichXenomorphRunner\Aldrich.XenomorphRunner.dll[/code]
[/list]
On the first launch the mod copies its data to [i]Documents\Foxy Voxel\Going Medieval\Mods\XenomorphRunner[/i], which is where the game reads mods from. If it does not show as active in the game's [b]Mods[/b] menu, enable [i]Aldrich - The Runner[/i] there once.

[size=4][b]Settings[/b][/size]
After the first launch, in [i]BepInEx\config\aldrich.*.cfg[/i].

[size=4][b]Uninstall[/b][/size]
Remove the mod in Vortex, or delete [i]BepInEx\plugins\AldrichXenomorphRunner[/i], and delete [i]Documents\Foxy Voxel\Going Medieval\Mods\XenomorphRunner[/i]. Anything already built or spawned with it disappears from saves that have it.

[size=4][b]Compatibility[/b][/size]
Works on its own. Plays well with the other Aldrich mods.
