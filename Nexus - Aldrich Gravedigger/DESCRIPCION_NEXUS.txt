[size=5][b]Aldrich - Gravedigger[/b][/size]
For the plague years and the lost sieges: somewhere to put the dead, and someone whose job it is.

[b]Tested on Going Medieval 1.1.19.[/b] English and Spanish texts.

[size=4][b]Features[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Mass Grave[/b] - one wide pit for six bodies. Cheap, quick to dig and grim to look at.
[*][b]Mass Pyre[/b] - its own building: stack three bodies and burn them in a single, longer fire. More fuel than one by one, but the settlement stays clean when the dead pile up.
[*][b]Gravedigger role[/b] - the settlement's sexton, answering to the chapel. Three levels.
[*][b]Gravedigger perk[/b] - corpses are work, not horror: nothing about them upsets this settler, and a life among the sick has left a body that shakes off disease faster.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Perk: Gravedigger[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Costs [b]3 points[/b] at character creation.
[*]Seeing, handling or smelling corpses never upsets them (no dead-body mood penalties, no stomach damage from carcasses or rot).
[*]Wounds close and health recovers [b]twice as fast[/b].
[*]Likes hauling and tending work; gets on well with [i]Callous[/i] settlers.
[/list]

[size=4][b]How it works[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Gravedigger role[/b] - needs a chapel. Level 1: +4 mood. Level 3 (Sexton of the chapel): +10 mood and +10% work speed. Losing the chapel suspends the role.
[*][b]Mass Grave[/b] - holds six bodies in one plot.
[*][b]Mass Pyre[/b] - three bodies per burn, one longer fire.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Requirements[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]BepInEx 5 (x64)[/b]. Vortex installs it for you with Going Medieval.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Installation[/b][/size]
[b]With Vortex:[/b] install it like any other mod.

[b]By hand:[/b]
[list=1]
[*]Install BepInEx 5 (x64) in the Going Medieval folder, if you have not already.
[*]Extract the archive into the Going Medieval folder (where [i]Going Medieval.exe[/i] is), so the file ends up at:
[code]Going Medieval\BepInEx\plugins\AldrichGravedigger\Aldrich.Gravedigger.dll[/code]
[/list]
On the first launch the mod copies its data to [i]Documents\Foxy Voxel\Going Medieval\Mods\Gravedigger[/i], which is where the game reads mods from. If it does not show as active in the game's [b]Mods[/b] menu, enable [i]Aldrich - Gravedigger[/i] there once.

[size=4][b]Uninstall[/b][/size]
Remove the mod in Vortex, or delete [i]BepInEx\plugins\AldrichGravedigger[/i], and delete [i]Documents\Foxy Voxel\Going Medieval\Mods\Gravedigger[/i]. Graves and pyres already built with it disappear from saves that have them.

[size=4][b]Compatibility[/b][/size]
Works on its own. Plays well with the other Aldrich mods.

[size=4][b]Problems[/b][/size]
Please post [i]Going Medieval\BepInEx\LogOutput.log[/i] - the lines that start with [i][data][/i] and [i]Aldrich - Gravedigger[/i] tell what happened.
